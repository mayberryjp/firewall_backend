## Paired path MTU/MSS diagnostics

### Summary and components

Implemented deliberate, short-lived tests between two Netcontrol firewalls,
with any suitable live IP-capable interface selectable rather than a VPN-only
workflow. Both operators supply a one-time secret and approve temporary
selected/ancestor MTU increases. Authenticated directional UDP packet-size and
fresh TCP MSS trials produce evidence, not automatic persistent tuning.

- [Core request/limits](../../crates/netcontrol-core/src/path_test.rs),
  [backend lifecycle](../../crates/netcontrol-core/src/lib.rs) and
  [engine](../../crates/netcontrol-core/src/engine.rs): strict API,
  authorization, mutation barriers and shutdown cleanup;
  [core tests](../../crates/netcontrol-core/src/engine_tests.rs).
- [Protocol worker/tests](../../crates/netcontrol-services/src/path_test.rs):
  in-process authentication, source/device/port reservation, bounded

- [SSH CLI](../../crates/netcontrold/src/cli.rs),

- [Frontend contract](../../FRONTEND_SPEC.md#paired-path-mtumss-tests),

### Decisions and current contract

Additive unreleased start/status/cancel API; recommended release impact:
**additive, next automatic calendar version**, not a hand-edited version.
Start/cancel requires `diagnostics.run`, `interfaces.write` and `firewall.write`
together; status requires `state.read`. No management key is exchanged,
pairing secret saved, configuration revision created or persistent MSS clamp
installed. See the canonical spec for exact fields/status codes.

Use explicit selected maximum and individually approved parent maxima, restore
only matching owned interfaces, retain busy state and intent after cleanup
failure, and recover before normal startup reconciliation. Arming is 30 seconds,
measurements at most 30 seconds, overall permission lifetime 90 seconds.
Permit expiry does not revoke established conntrack acceptance; no unrelated
connections are flushed. A killed daemon must restart to restore MTUs.

Current preparation refuses configured DHCP clients and enabled BGP because
their asynchronous writers invalidate stable ownership, and affected/unresolved
dynamic WireGuard DNS paths. Do not stop services or guess underlays silently.
Numeric-endpoint tunnels remain eligible. Finite sampling (up to 12 IP sizes,
three MSS trials/direction), capped goodput, inner-tunnel fragmentation,
missing peer reports and endpoint-versus-forwarding limits remain explicit.
`path_mtu_bytes` is deliberately null, not an asserted exact boundary.
