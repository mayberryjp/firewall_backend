### Decisions and contract impact

configured subnet union. Missing family prefixes fail closed for ordinary IP;

protection, Linux uRPF, automatic tuning or a WAN return-traffic workaround.

ordinary rules and temporary path-test permits cannot bypass the source check.

the guard to normal policy; they never grant a final accept. Existing service
permits remain required. Earlier jump-based implementation was superseded
before completion because diagnostic permits prepend into normal chains.

Expose system-owned drop/return accounting and `reason:"spoof_block"` with
null compiled index, retaining normal capture, filtering, retention, Discord

and startup compiler refresh; no migration, connection flush or state reset.
